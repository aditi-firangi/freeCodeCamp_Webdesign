HER LEGACY

A mind ahead of its time

Augusta Ada King, Countess of Lovelace, was an English mathematician and writer whose ideas went far beyond the technology available during her lifetime.

Born in 1815, Ada developed a strong interest in mathematics and science. Her intellectual curiosity eventually led her to collaborate with inventor Charles Babbage and study his proposed Analytical Engine.

1815

Ada is born

Ada Lovelace was born in London on December 10, the daughter of poet Lord Byron and Anne Isabella Milbanke.

1833

Meets Charles Babbage

Ada met Charles Babbage and became fascinated by his ideas for mechanical calculating machines.

1843

The famous notes

Ada translated an article about Babbage's Analytical Engine and added extensive notes of her own.

1843

An algorithm is published

One of Ada's notes described a method for calculating Bernoulli numbers using the Analytical Engine.

1852

A lasting legacy

Ada died at the age of 36, but her ideas later became an important part of the history of computer science.
